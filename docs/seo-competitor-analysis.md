# SEO — SDM competitor analysis & topic-cluster strategy

Internal working note. Source: live crawl of `sdm.fi` sitemaps, 2026-08-28.

SDM is a high-volume generalist. Competing on volume is a losing game — the play
is product-led topical depth on the few clusters where we have a moat.

## What SDM covers (sampled titles)

Brand building, brand renewal, digital brand strategy, B2B brand, content
marketing audit, keyword research, Google Ads, Facebook buyer's guide, video
specs, Salesforce/Pardot, AI-optimisation guide (1x), employer branding.

Sampled word counts: 925–1284 words per post. **Their content is not thin — it
is generic.** Definitional, breadth-first coverage ("what is X", "why X
matters") across every topic, with no product and no measurable-outcome story.

## SDM's weakness → our angle

1. **No product story.** SDM sells services, not a product. NØRR3 has the
   Marketing Engine — a concrete thing with a measurable outcome.
2. **No measurement identity.** "jokainen mediaeuro mitataan" is a claim SDM
   cannot make as sharply; their content is process, not proof.
3. **Generic AI posture.** One AI guide vs. a whole AI-assisted marketing
   product.

## Topic clusters to own (priority order)

1. **Markkinoinnin automaatio / Marketing Engine** — product-led cluster.
   Pillar: "markkinoinnin automaatio". Spokes: paikallismarkkinointi,
   kampanjasuunnitelma, DCO/dynaaminen mainonta, monipaikkaisen ketjun
   markkinointi. (SDM has nothing here.)
2. **Ohjelmallinen ostaminen + paikallismarkkinointi** — high-intent, difficulty
   0 keywords (ohjelmallinen ostaminen 350/mo, dooh 60/mo, mainostoimisto).
3. **Mediasuunnittelu ja -mittaus** — data → media → todentaminen. Target
   "mediatoimisto Helsinki", "mediasuunnittelu", "konversiomittaus".
4. **GEO / AI-discoverability** — own the future moat before SDM notices.
   Quarter the answer-block work on the service pages.

## Immediate moves

- Continue the Marketing Engine content series (pillar + spokes) — started with
  `/markkinoinnin-automaatio`.
- Deepen the 5 service pages with industry-specific solution sections.
- Build the programmatic landing pages from Marketing Engine data (see the
  spec) — this is the volume answer that doesn't require 812 hand-written posts.
